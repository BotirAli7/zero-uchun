Soldier sprites
  Rendered from the rigged "Soldier.glb" model shipped with the three.js examples
  (https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r170/examples/models/gltf). The character
  ("Vanguard") and its Idle/Walk/Run animations come from Adobe Mixamo. Weapons,
  arm poses (IK), recolouring and the top-down render are done by tools/soldier_render.
  Before a commercial release, download the same character from mixamo.com with your
  own (free) Adobe account and re-render, so the licence is in your name.

Ground, road, floor and crate textures (web/assets/tex)
  ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), CC0 1.0:
  Asphalt022, Grass004, Ground037, Concrete034, WoodFloor041, Tiles074.
