3D soldier
  "Soldier.glb" from the three.js examples (https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r170/examples/models/gltf).
  The character ("Vanguard") and its Idle/Walk/Run animations come from Adobe Mixamo. Weapons, arm poses (IK)
  and faction tints are added at runtime by web/r3d.js. Before a commercial release, download the same
  character from mixamo.com with your own (free) Adobe account so the licence is in your name.

3D props (web/assets/models) - Poly Haven, CC0 1.0 (https://polyhaven.com/license)
  covered_car, concrete_road_barrier, street_lamp_01, Barrel_01, wooden_crate_01, ammo_box, metal_office_desk,
  Sofa_01, steel_frame_shelves_01, old_bed_frame, utility_box_01, metal_trash_can, portable_generator, shrub_03,
  old_tyre, cardboard_box_01, WoodenChair_01. Converted with gltf-transform (512 px WebP textures) and
  stored as self-contained glTF JSON (tools/glb_to_json.py).

three.js r170 (web/vendor/three), MIT licence (web/vendor/three/LICENSE.txt).
